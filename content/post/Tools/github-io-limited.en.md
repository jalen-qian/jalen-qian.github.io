> Recently, I found that none of my bookmarked github.io pages were accessible. At first, I thought they had been blocked, but I later discovered that the issue could be resolved by changing the network settings.
>
> Without further ado, here is the solution:

# Solution

## 1. Open Control Panel\Network and Internet\Network and Sharing Center

<img src="/images/QQ截图20201004133718.png" width="500" height="30" align="middle" />

## 2. Click “Properties”

<img src="/images/202010041337182323.png" width="300" height="30" align="middle" />

## 3. Double-click IPv4

<img src="/images/20201004183622.png" width="300" height="30" align="middle" />

## 4. Enter the DNS Server

First, select **Use the following DNS server addresses**.

Then enter **223.5.5.5.5**.

<img src="/images/20201004183647.png" width="300" height="30" align="middle" />

## 5. Open the Web Page and Check Whether It Is Accessible
